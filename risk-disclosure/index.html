General notice

This website discusses crypto-assets and decentralized technologies for educational purposes. Crypto-assets are high-risk instruments. You can lose the entire amount you commit. Nothing here is investment advice or a recommendation.

Volatility risk

Prices of DOT and other crypto-assets can change dramatically within short periods and may be affected by factors outside any single project's control, including liquidity conditions, macroeconomic events, and market sentiment.

Technology risk

Smart contracts, oracle networks, bridges, and wallets are software and can fail or be exploited. Transactions on public blockchains are generally irreversible; there is no deposit insurance and usually no recourse.

Oracle and data risk

Applications that rely on external data inherit the risks of that data pipeline: stale updates, source manipulation, or operator failure can produce incorrect on-chain outcomes.

Market and liquidity risk

You may be unable to buy or sell an asset at the expected price or at all, particularly in stressed or thin markets.

Regulatory risk

Laws and regulations applicable to crypto-assets vary by jurisdiction and may change, potentially affecting the legality, taxation, or availability of related products and services where you live.

Loss of access

Losing private keys or credentials typically means permanent loss of the associated assets. Phishing and malicious applications are common; verify everything independently.

Your responsibility

Consult a licensed financial, legal, or tax professional before making decisions. If anything on this site is unclear, contact us at editorial@dot-oracle.guide.
