Risks & Disclaimer

This content is educational and not financial advice. Read these risk categories before interacting with any crypto-asset or protocol.

Volatility Risk

Prices of DOT and other crypto-assets can move sharply in either direction within minutes, driven by liquidity, sentiment, macro events, or protocol news. Historical drawdowns of more than 80% from peak values have occurred. Never commit funds you cannot afford to lose entirely.

Smart Contract Risk

Smart contracts and oracle code can contain bugs despite audits. Exploits may drain funds irreversibly, and there is generally no chargeback, deposit insurance, or central party able to reverse transactions.

Market & Liquidity Risk

Thin order books can make it impossible to exit a position near the quoted price. Oracle feeds sourced from illiquid markets are also easier to manipulate, which has been a root cause of several DeFi incidents.

Regulatory Uncertainty

The legal treatment of crypto-assets, staking, and oracle services differs across jurisdictions and continues to evolve. Rules on taxation, custody, and marketing may change and could affect the availability or value of related services.

Oracle-Specific Risk

Data feeds can fail, lag, or report manipulated values if underlying sources are compromised. Applications inherit the trust assumptions of every oracle they consume.

Operational Risk

Lost keys, phishing, malicious front-ends, and user error account for a large share of real-world losses. Verify URLs and contracts independently.
