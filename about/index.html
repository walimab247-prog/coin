About DOT Oracle Guide

An independent educational project explaining oracle infrastructure in the Polkadot ecosystem.

Editorial Mission

Our mission is to make oracle technology understandable without hype. We explain how data reaches blockchains, where it is used, and what can go wrong — in neutral, verifiable language. We never publish price predictions, profit promises, or calls to buy any asset.

How Content Is Produced

  • Articles are original rewrites based on public documentation and reputable primary sources, never verbatim copies.
  • Every news item lists its sources, publication date, last-updated date, and author attribution.
  • Factual corrections are applied promptly and reflected in the “Updated” date.
  • Educational pages are reviewed periodically against current Polkadot documentation.

Transparency Statement
