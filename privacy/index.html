Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Scope

This policy describes how DOT Oracle Guide (“we”) handles information when you visit this website. We are an educational publication; we do not operate user accounts, payments, or trading functionality.

What we collect

  • Contact form and email: name, email address, and message content you choose to send, used solely to respond to you.
  • Newsletter form: the email address you submit, used only to send the updates you requested.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider may record standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested pages) for security and reliability.

Cookies and local storage

We set a single local-storage flag to remember your cookie-consent choice. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or analytics profiles. If third-party embedded content is ever added, it will be listed here.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries sent through the contact form or by email.
  • To deliver newsletter updates you explicitly requested.
  • To maintain the security and availability of the website.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Data is shared only with infrastructure providers strictly necessary to operate the site (e.g., hosting), or where required by law.

Retention and your rights

Correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may unsubscribe from updates at any time, by emailing editorial@dot-oracle.guide.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.
